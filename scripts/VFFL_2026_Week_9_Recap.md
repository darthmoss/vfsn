Ladies and gentlemen, grab a seat and pour something stiff, because Week 9 of the 2026 VFFL regular season just delivered an absolute masterclass in November chaos across the Red and Black Divisions! We are officially deep in the playoff push where every fractional point carries postseason life-or-death stakes, and this slate gave us everything from a nuclear 190.15-point coronation in the Black Division to a 2.53-point heart-stopper in the Red Division that was decided by a half-point running back crater.
